Imagine the categories as three trays on an analyst's desk. A useful assistant should put relevant paragraphs in the right tray without filling that tray with unrelated material. The transformer places **243 of 320 paragraphs** in the dataset's tray. That is better than the classical system, but it leaves **77 disagreements** for a person to resolve. These are matches to expert-provided labels, not checks on whether the company's underlying environmental claims are true. Evidence: `tables/test_metrics.csv` and `tables/paired_error_examples.csv`.

The opportunity tray is the clearest weakness. The transformer finds **27 of 51** paragraphs labelled opportunity. The other **24** are put in neutral. An analyst who reads only the opportunity tray could therefore miss almost half of the opportunities represented by this test's labels. It also misses **15 risk paragraphs**, so neutral or opportunity must not be treated as permission to disregard a paragraph. The confusion matrices in Section 8 give those counts directly.

The balanced score of **0.719** gives the three trays equal weight. It is below the proposed **0.80** target because the opportunity tray still has both missed cases and false alarms; Section 7.2 shows the arithmetic. The target is a project decision rule, not a universal boundary between a useful and a useless system. Failing it means that the model has not earned the level of reliance proposed for this pilot. A supervised trial might still test whether suggestions make analysts faster, but no user-time or business-impact experiment has been run.

Raising the model's acceptance threshold does not solve the workload trade-off in the measured validation results. For seed 42, the 0.80 threshold retains **116/200 paragraphs**, with **109/116 correct** (93.97%), below the proposed 95% target. Raising the threshold to 0.90 gives **31/31 correct** in validation but retains only **15.5%**, below the proposed 50% coverage target. This finite set of successful validation examples does not guarantee future correctness. Because neither threshold meets all configured requirements, the implemented policy sends every paragraph to review; it does not silently relax the targets. Evidence: `tables/transformer_seed_42_routing_validation.csv` and `tables/routing_test.csv`.

The business choice is therefore conditional. Keep the transformer as the candidate for an analyst-assistance trial because its paired score improves and it corrects some classical errors, while retaining the cheaper classical model as a comparison. Do not authorize unattended filtering, company ratings or environmental claims on this evidence. Reconsider the choice if a separately labelled test set from companies or reporting periods not seen during development clears the quality gates, reviewers can handle the measured queue, and a user study shows useful time savings without more missed risks. These are proposed conditions for further use, not results already demonstrated.
